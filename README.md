### CHEM-000301: Complete native heparan-sulfate chain sequencing
- **Subfield:** Round 2
- **Open:** Recover an individual native chain’s ordered sugars, uronate epimers and sulfation sites, retaining fragment provenance across long domains and rare 3-O-sulfated motifs.
- **Status:** The 2020 SIMMS² method used 36 synthetic di- to decasaccharide standards and sequenced natural hexasaccharides. Complete native-chain reconstruction remains beyond that fragment-level result.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Makes the proposed heparan-sulfate sequence code directly readable.
- **Key works:** R. L. Miller et al. (2020), *Shotgun ion mobility mass spectrometry sequencing of heparan sulfate saccharides*, [DOI:10.1038/s41467-020-15284-y](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-15284-y).
- **Related:** Bioinformatics: sequence assembly from ambiguous repeated fragments.
